BADGES LEGEND

SYSTEM ROLES
A job, title, type, or otherwise descriptive label for an alter based on what they do for the system. Multiple alters can fill the same role, especially ones that are difficult, by taking turns or fulfilling different parts of the task.
abuse taker abuse
An alter who primarily fronts or shows up specifically in stressful situations to handle, absorb, or disperse things like stress and anxiety, as well as trauma responses.
caretaker care
An alter who has the job of making sure other alters are taking care of themselves, or forcibly taking care of them. This is often but not always the type of alter who looks after age-sliders, age-regressors, littles, or middles.
gatekeeper keeper gate
An internal alter that controls who is allowed to front, usually for safety concerns. Sometimes also referred to as a bouncer or security.
host cohost
The alter(s) who are in front most often for day-to-day life, whether because it is their job, because they have things they need to do, or because they enjoy it. This sometimes coincides with the manager role, but not always.
internal self help internal ish
Internal Self Help is a broad classification for alters whose responsibilities are on the inside rather than related to fronting, often to take care of other alters or organize information.
manager
The alter who is in charge of checking in with and managing the activities and health of all the other alters. Generally they are the one who assigns tasks and roles, and often are the one to make big decisions for the system.
memory holder memory
An alter whose main purpose is generally to hold on to specific memories, thoughts, and feelings. This allows other alters to be less burdened with knowledge, and the others may not even know about the memories.
persecutor
An alter whose purpose is to nitpick, bully, or cause harm to other alters for the overall health of the system. Many do not understand how important persecutors are for a system.
protector
A form of abuse taker, these alters come to front specifically to defend the system, whether it be physically or psychologically. Sometimes referred to as a guard dog or emergency fronter.
traumaholder trauma
A form of memory holder whose primary purpose is to hold on to traumatic memories so that the others are not forced to relive them, but they are not forgotten and can still be learned from.
ORIGIN TYPES
The way an alter was formed, or the type of alter they identify themself as. This is not always black and white, as alters can split and merge and this sometimes confuses the origin.
absorbed
An alter that has merged with or become part of at least one other non-fragment alter, often multiple, without becoming a subsystem and instead turning into one entity.
concocted
An alter that was not directly formed by one or more outside influence enough to consider it their identity. They may still have been influenced or have exomemories, but not to a the extent of becoming that person.
core
The original, singular alter who was in or claimed the body first. Not every system has one, or some don't remember who it was.
factive
An introject that formed with the identity and/or exomemories of a real person, animal, or other non-fictional conscious entity.
fictive
An introject that formed with the identity and/or exomemories of a fictional person, animal, creature, or other existing created conscious entity.
fragment
An alter or pseudoalter that is not currently fully formed. They may not have ever formed properly, or they may have split apart into something they can no longer hold together. Often they find it hard to exist outside of the presence of a specific alter.
introject
An alter that formed with the personality, identity, exomemories, or direct influence of an existing entity. They do not always reflect their source and making assumptions about them based on it can be harmful.
mirror
A type of factive who specifically formed with the influence, identity, personality, or exomemories of an existing alter in an external system.
split
An alter or set of alters that had once been a single entity and have since split into multiple, whether they are fully-functioning or fragments. Sometimes this effect is not metaphysical, and the split alters remain in one internal "body" in headspace, leading to a subsystem.
DESCRIPTIVE
A descriptive label for an alter based on how they behave or how they affect the system, without it necessarily being something they are tasked to do.
abuser
An alter who is currently or has in the past been abusive towards other members of the system, sometimes an introject of the system's external abuser.
age-regressor regressor agere
An alter who is metaphysically an adult, but will regress into a younger mental state, often as a response to trauma or stress.
age-slider slider
An alter who is by age an adult, but will regress into a younger metaphysical and mental state, often uncontrollably.
animal
An alter who is mentally and metaphysically a non-anthropomorphic animal, often with animal-level intelligence and/or communication ability.
hallucinogenic
An alter who, when fronting, frequently causes the body to experience at least one type of sensory hallucination.
little
An alter who is mentally and metaphysically a child, generally up to the age of 11 or 12. Their cognition, access to memory, and communication ability may vary.
middle
An alter who is mentally and metaphysically older than a child but not an adult, generally between the ages of 13-17. They may not have full access to system information, but often know more than the littles.
nonverbal semiverbal mute nv sv
An alter who consistently cannot, may not, will not, or does not communicate fully in one or more ways. This can extend to speech, text, emojis, drawings, and sign language, and they may use limited or none of these options.
subsystem
A type of split that did not result in multiple alters having their own separate metaphysical internal "bodies" in headspace, leading to a secondary system fully enclosed within one of the alters of the larger main system.
